# Kev: Linkyoh's AI avatar

Owner decision: Cristian, 2026-09-13 (Belize), recorded 2026-09-14 UTC.
The man in the approved local preview is named **Kev** and may be reused in
Linkyoh advertisements, flyers and marketing materials. This authorizes retaining
the character, not activating an advertising campaign.

- Appearance: short curly dark/salt-and-pepper hair, neatly trimmed facial hair,
  navy work shirt, approachable and practical expression.

Preserve this master unchanged. Use it as the image reference for future poses,
service contexts and formats so Kev remains visually consistent. Adapt derivatives
for their placement without stretching, cutting off the face or obscuring text.
Use the current Linkyoh Lab brand and approved wordmark.

## Identity and use

Kev is a fictional, AI-generated brand character and guide to local services.
He is not a real Belize provider, licensed plumber, customer or employee. Do not
create a provider record, contact number, review, rating or Checked badge for him.
Do not imply that illustrative repairs are work performed by an actual business.
Real providers retain their own photos, service areas and verification records.

The plumbing scene may illustrate discovery of plumbing/home-repair services.
Other trades can be represented in future reviewed artwork without inventing
qualifications or availability. Use appropriate tools, work practices and context.
Do not invent testimonial quotes or promise work by Kev.

Suggested disclosure when introducing the character or when imagery could be
mistaken for an actual professional:

- EN: "Kev, Linkyoh's AI-generated guide to local services."
- ES: "Kev, el guia de Linkyoh creado con IA para encontrar servicios locales."

Before any campaign, review the artwork, factual service claims, disclosure,
destination, targeting and budget. This release does not publish an ad, contact a
provider, activate WOP or enroll a user.
